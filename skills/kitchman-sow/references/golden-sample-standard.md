# Golden Sample Output Standard

Use human-written Kitchman proposals as output quality standards only. They are not runtime inputs for a new SOW unless the user uploads them as project materials for that specific job.

## Allowed Uses

- Mirror the proposal structure, section order, business tone, and table-first style.
- Check whether the generated proposal looks like a formal Kitchman client document rather than an internal analysis memo.
- Compare coverage: header, client letter, documents provided, working areas, finish schedule, production/delivery/installation terms, exclusions, lead time, payment terms, and sign-off language.

## Forbidden Uses

Do not copy project facts from a golden sample into a new project unless the same facts appear in the current user's message or uploaded current-project files.

Examples of forbidden carry-over facts:

- Client names.
- Quote IDs or revisions.
- Proposal dates.
- Final prices, GST, or grand totals.
- Specific material codes, finishes, hardware brands, lead times, and payment terms.
- Room scope decisions that were specific to the golden sample project.

## Target Proposal Shape

A customer-facing Kitchman proposal should look like a formal quote/proposal letter:

1. Recipient, date, quote ID, project, and site.
2. Short greeting and proposal introduction.
3. Design and planning scope.
4. Documents provided by client table.
5. Joinery working areas table.
6. TBC items table for visible but unconfirmed scope or supplier responsibilities.
7. Joinery finish schedule table.
8. Shop drawing, site remeasure, production, delivery, and installation terms.
9. Pricing placeholder or estimator-completed pricing.
10. Exclusions.
11. Lead time and payment terms placeholders when not confirmed.
12. Closing and signature block.

## Demo Behavior

For demo output, keep the formal shape but use safe placeholders:

- `TBC` for unknown client, quote, finish, hardware, lead time, and payment terms.
- `Estimator to complete` for pricing.
- `Not included unless confirmed by revision` for unclear exclusions.

Keep internal reasoning, visual observations, and risk notes in `open-questions.md` or review notes, not in the client-facing proposal body.

## v0.4 Quality Checks

- Confirmed working-area rows do not contain responsibility-TBC items.
- Stone, LED, mirrors, glass, appliances, plumbing, electrical works, and door structure responsibilities appear in `TBC Items`, `Exclusions`, or `Items Requiring Confirmation` unless explicitly confirmed.
- The delivered ZIP contains `sow-extraction.json`, `proposal.md`, `proposal.docx`, `open-questions.md`, and `feishu-summary.md`.
- The DOCX includes Kitchman logo header/footer parts when generated by the renderer.
